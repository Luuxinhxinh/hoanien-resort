Tours & Experiences

Immerse yourself in local crafts, historic architecture, and raw nature with curated excursions designed exclusively for Hoanien's guests.

Connect with Hoi An's Soul

Our tours go beyond standard sightseeing. We pair you with local fishermen, historic preservationists, and organic horticulturists to let you experience the authentic heart of our heritage river land.

Signature Previews

Sunset River

Thu Bồn River Sunset Cruise

Drift along the calm currents as the sun sets over traditional fishing villages. Features local tea, seafood snacks, and story sharing with veteran river navigators.

Eco Culture

Cam Thanh Village Heritage Ride

Cycle through verdant rice paddies, meeting bamboo craftsmen and exploring the coconut forest waters in a traditional bamboo basket boat.

Architecture

Hoi An Ancient Town Architecture Walk

Walk the yellow alleys accompanied by a local architect. Enter closed-door private heritage homes and discover the blended Japanese, Chinese, and French styles.
