Food to Nourish — Hoi An Dining

Dining

Locally sourced ingredients, traditional flavors, and modern techniques combine to create a culinary journey that celebrates the rich heritage of Hoi An and the vibrant spirit of Vietnam.

Dine with Kawaii

Our culinary philosophy is rooted in deep respect for local farmers, fishermen, and artisans. Every dish tells a story of the region's abundant harvest, crafted to nourish both body and soul.

The Merchant

Inspired by the bustling trading ports of ancient Hoi An, The Merchant offers a vibrant all-day dining experience. Journey through interactive kitchens showcasing authentic Vietnamese delicacies and international favorites.

Opening Hours: 06:30 - 22:30

Style: All-Day Dining & Local Specialties

The Fisherman

Savor the catch of the day at our riverside grill. The Fisherman celebrates the rich maritime heritage of the central coast, offering expertly prepared seafood in an intimate, open-air setting by the Thu Bon river.

Opening Hours: 17:00 - 23:00

Style: Fresh Seafood & Signature Grill

River Cruise

Experience the magic of Hoi An from the water. Step aboard our traditional wooden boat for a sunset cocktail cruise or a romantic dinner under the stars, drifting gently along the Thu Bon River.

Schedule: Sunset (16:30) & Dinner (19:00)

Style: Exclusive Floating Experience
