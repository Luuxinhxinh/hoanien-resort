Mindful Ways To Discover Hoi An

Experiences

Step off the beaten path and immerse yourself in the rich tapestry of Hoi An's culture, crafts, and natural landscapes. Meaningful journeys await.

Curated Local Encounters

We believe travel should be transformative. Our bespoke experiences are designed to connect you deeply with the local community, honoring their traditions while creating unforgettable memories.

Paddy Field Cycling

Take a gentle ride through the lush, emerald rice paddies surrounding the retreat. Feel the morning breeze, wave to local farmers, and witness the tranquil rural life that remains unchanged by time.

Duration: 2 Hours

Activity Level: Easy

Heritage Craft Workshops

Sit down with generations-old artisans and learn the delicate art of lantern making or traditional pottery. Take home not just a souvenir, but a piece of Hoi An's living heritage crafted by your own hands.

Duration: 1.5 Hours

Location: Artisan Village / Retreat Hub

River Ecosystem Tour

Board a traditional bamboo basket boat to navigate the intricate nipa palm forests. Learn the unique net-casting techniques from local fishermen and immerse yourself in the vibrant river ecosystem.

Duration: 3 Hours

Best Time: Early Morning or Late Afternoon
